# HITS transport reference data

GateLog keeps HITS transport references separate from operational buses and vehicles. The checked-in snapshot contains 23 route numbers and names, bus names, ordered boarding points, morning times, and campus endpoints from the official [HITS Transportation page](https://hindustanuniv.ac.in/facilities/transport/). It records the timetable label shown on that page, **2023–2024**, and is marked `reference_only`. No driver or in-charge personal names, contacts, or fees are copied into the dataset.

The snapshot's `fetched_at` is the UTC retrieval time, not the page's publication date. Because the source timetable is historical, confirm current route and timing details with HITS before operational use. Times are normalized to `HH:MM` from the source's A.M. columns. The page includes two consistent copies of each table, which the importer deduplicates; route 19A and route 09's unusual label are preserved.

From `backend`, run `python -m app.database.transport --refresh` to fetch the official page, validate the timetable, and atomically replace the checked-in snapshot. It retries fetch or parse failures up to three times (`--attempts` supports 1–5) and leaves the existing snapshot intact when validation fails, including a decreased route count. No extra parser package is required. Route records are upserted by `hits-route-<number>` into `transport_routes`; they never create operational bus or vehicle records. Buses link to an official route by route number when a reference exists. Custom operational routes remain explicitly unlinked.
